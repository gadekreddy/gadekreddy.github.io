About Trinadh Industries

Who We Are

Trinadh Industries is a leading manufacturer and supplier of Pharmaceutical APIs (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients), KSMs (Key Starting Materials), and Intermediates. Located in the heart of Hyderabad's pharmaceutical hub at IDA Nacharam, we are committed to delivering high-quality products that meet international standards.

Our Mission

To provide superior quality pharmaceutical ingredients while maintaining the highest standards of safety, efficacy, and regulatory compliance. We strive to be a trusted partner for pharmaceutical companies worldwide.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized leader in pharmaceutical manufacturing, known for innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction.
